## robustness.png / .pdf

Claim 3. Seeds 3000-3199, two sites, results at 04eed15 plus `hpc/sweeps/phase_d`. Top row: pla broadcasts confident wrong beliefs; bottom: resin does. Left: trust per round (the trust applied to that round's messages): the lying sender's falls to 0.03-0.04, the honest sender's stays above 0.9. Right: the honest site's error, log scale. Under attack (orange dotted) networked jumps to 74-93 µm in round 1, then comes back to about isolated by round 3, but not to networked without the attack, and stays above isolated when resin lies (17.6 vs 14.6 µm). 103/400 attacked runs are still harmed beyond noise at round 3.

## ablation.png / .pdf

The same 200 seeds (3000-3199), two sites, generated at d0bdbc3. Left: noise-free held-out error per round for isolated, networked with the network cut (gray, "no messages"), networked and oracle. Right: each arm's paired gain over isolated with 95% CIs. Networked gains 1.6-1.8 µm in every round; with no messages the gain is 0.1-0.3 µm (CI includes 0), so the benefit is the messages, not the agent auction.
